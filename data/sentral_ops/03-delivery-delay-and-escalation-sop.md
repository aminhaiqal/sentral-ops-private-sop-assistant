# Delivery Delay and Escalation SOP

**Owner:** Operations Manager  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-05-12  
**Classification:** Internal Use Only  

## Purpose

This SOP defines how Sentral Ops handles delivery delays, failed deliveries, partial deliveries, and customer updates. It is intended for Customer Support, Operations Coordinators, and Delivery Leads.

## Standard Delivery Windows

Normal scheduled deliveries are planned in either a morning window from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM or an afternoon window from 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM. Urgent orders may be assigned a tighter window only when Operations confirms driver capacity and stock readiness.

Support must not promise an exact arrival time unless Operations has confirmed it for that specific order.

## Definitions

An at-risk delivery is one that may miss the promised window due to driver delay, picking delay, stock exception, routing issue, or customer access problem.

A delayed delivery is one that has missed the promised delivery window but can still be completed on the same day.

A failed delivery is one that cannot be completed because the customer was unavailable, the address was inaccessible, the item was not ready, the driver could not complete the route, or the customer rejected delivery.

A partial delivery is one where only part of the confirmed order is delivered. Partial delivery may be planned or unplanned and must be recorded clearly.

## Escalation Levels

Level 1 is handled by Customer Support and the Operations Coordinator. It covers routine delay checks, customer updates, and internal notes.

Level 2 is handled by the Delivery Lead or Support Lead. It covers delayed deliveries over 60 minutes, customer dissatisfaction, unclear driver notes, or repeated issue history.

Level 3 is handled by the Operations Manager. It covers failed delivery, urgent clinic or production-impact orders, high-value account complaints, unresolved routing issues, or any delay likely to affect the next business day.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate to the Support Lead or Delivery Lead when:

- The delivery is more than 60 minutes at risk.
- The customer has contacted Support twice about the same delivery.
- Driver notes are missing, unclear, or contradict the order record.
- A partial delivery was made without prior customer agreement.
- The item is for a clinic, warehouse operation, or other time-sensitive environment.

Escalate to the Operations Manager when:

- A delivery has failed or is likely to fail.
- The delay is more than two hours beyond the promised window.
- The customer requests cancellation, refund, or senior escalation because of the delay.
- A driver safety, vehicle breakdown, or access restriction issue affects multiple orders.
- The order has special pricing, special handling, or management visibility.

## Customer Update Templates

At-risk delivery:

"We are checking the current delivery progress with Operations. The order is still active, but there may be a delay against the planned window. We will update you once the delivery team confirms the next reliable status."

Delayed delivery:

"We apologise for the delay. Operations is reviewing the route and driver update now. We will provide the next confirmed update as soon as it is available."

Failed delivery:

"We were unable to complete the delivery as planned. Our Operations team is reviewing the reason and the next available option. We will update you once the recovery plan is confirmed."

## Internal Notes Required

Every delay case must include the order number, customer name, promised delivery window, latest driver or route note, customer contact history, staff member handling the case, and the next owner.

If the ETA is uncertain, the internal note must say "ETA not yet confirmed" rather than guessing.

## AI Assistant Notes

The assistant may explain delay definitions, escalation levels, escalation triggers, and update templates. It must not invent a live ETA, driver location, dispatch status, or delivery completion status. For questions such as "What is today's delivery ETA?", the assistant should say that live ETA must be checked with Operations or the delivery system.
