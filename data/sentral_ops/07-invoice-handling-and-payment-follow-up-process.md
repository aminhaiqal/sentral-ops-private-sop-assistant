# Invoice Handling and Payment Follow-Up Process

**Owner:** Finance/Admin Manager  
**Version:** 1.5  
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-05-16  
**Classification:** Internal Use Only  

## Purpose

This process explains when invoices are created, what information is required, how payment follow-up is handled, and what staff may say about payment status.

## Invoice Creation Triggers

An invoice may be created when goods are delivered, when a recurring account order is confirmed under approved billing terms, when a service recovery adjustment has been approved, or when Finance/Admin confirms that billing should proceed.

For cash-before-delivery customers, invoice or receipt handling follows the payment terms set by Finance/Admin.

## Required Invoice Information

Finance/Admin requires:

- Customer legal or trading name.
- Account code and billing contact.
- Delivery address or service location.
- Order number and delivery note reference.
- Product description, SKU where available, quantity, unit price, discount, and tax treatment where applicable.
- Approved special pricing reference, if any.
- Payment terms.
- Staff member or system record that triggered billing.

Invoices should not be created from incomplete chat messages. Finance/Admin must be able to trace the billing record to the order and delivery or approved billing trigger.

## Payment Terms

Payment terms vary by account. Common terms include cash before delivery, seven days, fourteen days, or thirty days from invoice date. Only Finance/Admin may confirm the terms for a specific customer account.

Staff should not promise extended terms without Finance/Admin and Sales approval.

## Follow-Up Schedule

For seven-day terms, Finance/Admin sends a polite reminder one day after due date if unpaid.

For fourteen-day terms, Finance/Admin checks the ledger on day 7, sends a reminder on day 10 if payment has not been recorded, and escalates internally on day 14 if payment remains outstanding.

For thirty-day terms, Finance/Admin reviews at day 21, reminds at day 28, and escalates at day 35 if unresolved.

When payment is 14 days overdue, Finance/Admin should check whether there is a dispute, missing invoice, delivery issue, credit note pending, or customer remittance not yet matched. If no valid blocker exists, Finance/Admin should notify Sales and the Finance/Admin Manager for account follow-up and possible credit hold review.

## Dispute Handling

If a customer disputes an invoice, Finance/Admin should record the dispute reason, check order and delivery records, involve Support or Operations if the issue concerns delivery, and avoid aggressive payment language until the dispute is reviewed.

## Credit Note Handling

Credit notes require a source reason and approval according to the refund and credit note SOP. Finance/Admin should link the credit note to the original invoice, approved case record, and approving manager.

## Payment Confirmation Rules

Only Finance/Admin may confirm payment status after checking approved bank, payment gateway, or ledger records. Screenshots from customers may be used as supporting evidence but are not final confirmation until matched to the company record.

Support, Sales, Operations, and the AI assistant must not tell a customer that payment is confirmed unless Finance/Admin has verified it.

## AI Assistant Notes

The assistant may explain invoice information requirements, payment terms in general, follow-up schedule, dispute handling, and credit note handling. It must not confirm whether a specific customer has paid, whether a receipt has cleared, or whether an account is on credit hold. For "Is customer ABC's payment confirmed?", the assistant should say Finance/Admin must verify the live payment record.
