# Company Profile and Operating Context

**Owner:** Management Office  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-05-18  
**Classification:** Internal Use Only  

## Business Summary

Sentral Ops Supply Sdn. Bhd. is a fictional B2B operations and facilities supply company based in Klang Valley, Malaysia. The company serves small and mid-sized operators that need reliable everyday supplies without maintaining a large procurement team.

The company has 42 employees across Sales, Operations, Customer Support, Finance/Admin, and Management. The operating model is practical and relationship-led: staff know customer preferences, delivery constraints, common substitutions, credit terms, and escalation patterns. This knowledge is useful, but it also creates risk when the rules live in scattered files or senior staff memory.

## Customer Segments

Sentral Ops supports:

- Clinics that purchase gloves, cleaning supplies, front-desk consumables, labels, and pantry items.
- Tuition centres that purchase stationery, cleaning products, printing paper, and classroom supplies.
- Small offices that purchase pantry, cleaning, stationery, and basic facilities items.
- Warehouses that purchase packing consumables, labels, safety supplies, and cleaning items.
- Cafes that purchase cleaning supplies, receipt paper, packaging support items, and staff pantry products.
- Light industrial operators that purchase maintenance consumables, safety items, and floor-care materials.

## Team Structure

Sales owns account relationships, recurring order plans, and special pricing requests. Operations owns picking, packing, delivery coordination, failed delivery review, and stock exceptions. Customer Support owns first response, customer updates, issue triage, and escalation quality. Finance/Admin owns invoices, payment follow-up, credit notes, and customer account records. Management owns final approval on high-risk exceptions, large refunds, and policy changes.

## Internal Knowledge Problem

SOPs, finance notes, support guides, product handling rules, and data policy are currently stored across shared drives, chat threads, spreadsheets, and the memories of senior staff. New staff often ask the same operational questions repeatedly. Experienced staff can answer quickly, but the answer may vary depending on who is available.

The private SOP assistant is intended to make approved knowledge easier to find without replacing manager judgment or live operational systems.

## Candidate AI Questions

The assistant should be useful for questions such as:

- What information is required before processing a refund?
- When should a delivery delay be escalated to the Operations Manager?
- What should Support check before escalating a missing item?
- What should new support staff learn in week one?
- When is product substitution allowed?
- What should Finance do when payment is 14 days overdue?

## Data Boundary Expectations

The assistant must answer from approved documents only. It must not confirm live delivery status, current stock availability, payment clearance, customer-specific account status, or final approval decisions. It may explain the SOP, cite the source document, and direct staff to the correct system owner.

If the assistant has no source for an answer, staff should treat the response as incomplete and check with the relevant SOP owner. Unsupported answers must not be used as approval, customer commitment, or finance instruction.

## AI Assistant Notes

The assistant may summarize the company context, team responsibilities, and the purpose of the private SOP assistant. It must not invent real customers, live orders, delivery ETAs, stock quantities, payment status, or management decisions.
