# Internal Support Troubleshooting Guide

**Owner:** Customer Support Lead  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-05-13  
**Classification:** Internal Use Only  

## Purpose

This guide helps Customer Support check common issues before escalating. Good escalation saves time because Operations, Finance/Admin, and Management receive the facts needed to decide quickly.

## Before Escalating Any Case

Support should check:

- Customer name and account code.
- Order number, invoice number, and delivery note if available.
- Customer's exact issue and requested outcome.
- Timeline of customer contact.
- Internal order status and latest staff note.
- Evidence such as photo, packing note, delivery note, driver note, invoice, or customer email.
- Whether the case is urgent, high-value, repeat, or related to hygiene, safety, or regulated use.
- Which SOP applies and which team owns the next decision.

Escalation notes should be factual. Do not write assumptions as confirmed facts.

## Order Not Received

Check order number, promised delivery window, delivery note, driver or route note, customer contact history, and whether the address or receiver details were complete. If the order is delayed or failed, use the delivery delay SOP.

## Missing Item

Before escalating a missing item, Support should check:

- Original order and confirmed quantity.
- Packing record or pick list.
- Delivery note and customer signed acceptance if available.
- Whether the item was intentionally split into a partial delivery.
- Whether a stock exception or substitution was recorded.
- Customer photo or unpacking note if provided.
- Whether the item is urgent for customer operations.

Escalate to Operations when the packing or delivery record does not explain the missing item. Escalate to Finance/Admin only if a refund, credit note, or invoice adjustment may be required.

## Wrong Item Delivered

Check the SKU, product name, packing record, customer photo, and whether a substitution was approved. If there is no approved substitution record, treat the issue as a wrong item case and escalate to Operations.

## Urgent Delivery

Support may mark urgency but must ask Operations to confirm stock readiness and route capacity. Do not promise same-day delivery without Operations confirmation.

## Invoice Issue

Check invoice number, order number, customer account, pricing reference, delivery note, and whether a credit note or dispute case exists. Escalate to Finance/Admin with the exact mismatch and supporting record.

## Payment Already Made

If a customer says payment was already made, ask for remittance information and forward the case to Finance/Admin. Support must not confirm payment status unless Finance/Admin verifies it.

## Special Pricing

Special pricing must have Sales Lead approval or an approved account record. Support should not apply informal discounts from chat memory.

## Angry Customer

Acknowledge the issue, avoid arguing, collect facts, and escalate if the customer requests manager review, threatens cancellation, has a repeated unresolved issue, or the case involves service failure.

## Escalation Quality Checklist

A strong escalation includes the customer, order, issue type, evidence, timeline, requested outcome, suspected SOP, urgency, and recommended next owner. A weak escalation says only "customer chasing" or "please check" without context.

## AI Assistant Notes

The assistant may explain troubleshooting checks and escalation quality requirements. It must not update customer records, confirm live delivery or payment status, decide refund outcomes, or replace the assigned case owner.
