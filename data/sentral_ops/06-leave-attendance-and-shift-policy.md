# Leave, Attendance, and Shift Policy

**Owner:** HR/Admin Coordinator  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-05-11  
**Classification:** Internal Use Only  

## Purpose

This policy sets expectations for working hours, attendance, leave requests, medical leave, emergency absence, shift swaps, and handover before leave.

## Working Hours

Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Operations and delivery support roles may follow assigned shift coverage depending on route volume and customer commitments.

Employees must follow the working schedule assigned by their manager. Changes to schedule must be approved before they are treated as valid.

## Attendance Expectations

Employees are expected to be ready to work at the start of their scheduled time. Repeated late arrival, missed check-ins, or unexplained absence must be reported to the manager and HR/Admin.

Attendance records should be accurate. Employees must not ask colleagues to mark attendance on their behalf.

## Lateness Reporting

If an employee expects to be late, they must notify their direct manager as soon as practical. The message should include expected arrival time, reason, and whether any customer-facing work needs temporary cover.

For Support and Operations roles, lateness must include a handover note if open customer cases, route checks, or dispatch updates are affected.

## Emergency Absence

Emergency absence should be reported to the direct manager as soon as possible. If the manager is unreachable and the absence affects same-day operations, the employee should notify the function lead or HR/Admin.

The employee must update the team once they are able to provide expected return timing and handover details.

## Annual Leave Request

Annual leave should be requested through the approved leave process at least five working days in advance where possible. Leave is subject to manager approval and operational coverage.

Leave is not confirmed until approved. Employees should not make customer commitments that depend on unapproved leave coverage.

## Medical Leave

Medical leave must be supported by appropriate documentation according to HR/Admin requirements. Employees should notify their manager as early as possible and provide handover information for urgent work.

## Shift Swap

Shift swaps require approval from the direct manager. Both employees involved must confirm the swap, and the manager must ensure coverage, skill fit, and workload balance.

Informal swaps through chat are not valid unless the manager confirms approval.

## Handover Before Leave

Before leave, employees should hand over open customer issues, pending approvals, delivery exception cases, invoice disputes, recurring order commitments, and any deadline-sensitive task.

The handover should name the backup owner and include links or references to the relevant case records.

## AI Assistant Notes

The assistant may explain leave request steps, attendance expectations, medical leave handling, shift swap rules, and handover requirements. It must not approve leave, judge medical documents, alter attendance records, or make HR decisions.
