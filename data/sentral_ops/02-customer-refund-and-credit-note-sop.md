# Customer Refund and Credit Note SOP

**Owner:** Finance/Admin Manager  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-05-10  
**Classification:** Internal Use Only  

## Purpose

This SOP explains how Sentral Ops handles customer refunds, replacements, and credit notes for damaged goods, missing items, wrong items, duplicate billing, and approved service recovery cases.

The goal is to resolve valid customer issues quickly while protecting margin, audit records, and approval discipline.

## Refund, Replacement, and Credit Note Definitions

A replacement is a resend of the same item or an approved equivalent item at no additional charge. It is preferred when the customer still needs the product and the issue is operationally clear.

A refund is money returned to the customer through the original payment channel or an approved finance channel. Refunds are used when replacement is not suitable, the customer rejects replacement, or the order was cancelled under eligible terms.

A credit note is a finance document that reduces the customer's outstanding balance or future invoice amount. Credit notes are preferred for account customers with recurring purchases, especially when the customer has not yet paid the affected invoice.

## Required Information Before Processing

Support must collect the following before a refund, replacement, or credit note is processed:

- Customer name and account code.
- Order number and invoice number, if issued.
- Product name, SKU if available, quantity affected, and unit price.
- Issue type: damaged, missing, wrong item, duplicate billing, cancelled order, pricing dispute, or other.
- Evidence such as photo, delivery note, driver note, packing record, or written customer explanation.
- Customer requested outcome: replacement, refund, credit note, or review.
- Staff member who received the complaint and date received.
- Whether the customer needs an urgent operational workaround.

If any required information is missing, Support may acknowledge the case but must not promise a refund amount or credit note.

## Eligibility Rules

Refunds or credit notes may be considered when:

- Goods were damaged before or during delivery and evidence supports the claim.
- The wrong item was delivered and the customer cannot use the item.
- A confirmed missing item cannot be resent within a reasonable operational window.
- A duplicate invoice or pricing error is verified by Finance/Admin.
- Management approves a service recovery exception.

Refunds or credit notes should normally be declined when:

- The customer changed their mind after accepting correct goods.
- The product was damaged after delivery due to customer handling.
- The customer cannot provide reasonable evidence and internal records do not support the claim.
- The item was a special-order, non-returnable item and the customer approved the order terms.

## Approval Matrix

Support staff may prepare the case file but may not approve refunds or credit notes independently.

For replacement of affected goods up to RM150, a Support Lead may approve when evidence is complete and the replacement matches the original order.

For refund, credit note, or replacement value from RM151 to RM750, approval is required from the Operations Manager and Finance/Admin review is required before issuing financial documents.

For refund or credit note value from RM751 to RM2,500, approval is required from both the Operations Manager and Finance/Admin Manager.

For refund or credit note value above RM2,500, General Manager approval is required after Finance/Admin Manager review.

No staff member may approve a refund, credit note, or replacement for a case they personally caused without manager review.

## Process Flow

1. Support logs the complaint and gathers required information.
2. Support checks order, invoice, delivery, packing, and customer communication records.
3. Operations confirms whether the issue was damaged goods, missing item, wrong item, failed delivery, or stock exception.
4. Finance/Admin confirms invoice status before any credit note or refund is issued.
5. The approving manager reviews value, evidence, customer history, and proposed outcome.
6. Support updates the customer only after the outcome is approved.
7. Finance/Admin issues the credit note or processes the refund where applicable.
8. Support closes the case with the source record, decision, approving person, and customer message.

## Communication Template

For approved replacement:

"Thank you for sending the details. We have reviewed the order record and approved a replacement for the affected item. Our team will update you with the delivery arrangement once Operations confirms the dispatch slot."

For pending review:

"Thank you for raising this. We are checking the order, delivery, and invoice records before confirming the next step. We will update you once the review is complete."

For declined refund:

"We have reviewed the available records and are unable to approve a refund for this case based on the current information. If you have additional evidence, please send it to us and we will reopen the review."

## AI Assistant Notes

The assistant may explain required refund information, eligibility rules, approval thresholds, and process steps. It must not approve a refund, promise a refund amount, confirm invoice status, or issue a credit note. For a RM2,000 refund, the assistant should state that approval from both the Operations Manager and Finance/Admin Manager is required and that the assistant cannot grant approval.
