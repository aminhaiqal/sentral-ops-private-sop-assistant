# Product and Service Knowledge FAQ

**Owner:** Sales Operations Lead  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-05-08  
**Classification:** Internal Use Only  

## Purpose

This FAQ gives staff a shared baseline for product and service questions. It does not replace supplier specifications, customer contracts, approved price lists, or manager approval.

## Product and Service Categories

Sentral Ops commonly supplies cleaning consumables, pantry supplies, paper products, stationery, packing materials, labels, basic safety items, clinic front-desk consumables, and light facilities supplies.

The service promise is reliability and operational fit rather than the lowest marketplace price. Staff should explain that account customers receive ordering support, consolidated billing where approved, issue handling, and practical substitution support.

## Marketplace Price Matching

Sentral Ops does not automatically match marketplace prices. Marketplace listings may exclude delivery, invoice support, account handling, quality consistency, minimum quantities, or service recovery. Sales may review price-sensitive accounts, but special pricing must be approved through the Sales Lead and recorded against the account.

Staff may say:

"We can review recurring usage and account pricing, but we do not automatically match marketplace listings because our service includes fulfilment support, billing support, and issue handling."

## Recurring Item Availability

Recurring item availability should be reviewed using the approved stock or purchasing channel. Staff may explain usual ordering patterns but must not promise current stock unless Operations or Purchasing has confirmed it.

If an item is commonly recurring but temporarily unavailable, Support should ask Operations whether there is an approved substitute, incoming stock date, or backorder option.

## Substitution Principles

Substitution is allowed only when the substitute is operationally suitable, not misleading, and approved according to risk level.

Low-risk substitutions include similar cleaning cloths, general office stationery, standard pantry items, or equivalent packaging items where brand is not material to use.

High-risk substitutions include clinic gloves, safety items, chemical products, food-contact items, size-specific items, customer-branded items, and anything affected by hygiene, safety, material specification, or regulatory expectation.

High-risk substitutions require customer approval before dispatch unless a manager has explicitly approved a documented emergency exception.

## Urgent Order Rules

Urgent orders are accepted only when stock readiness and delivery capacity are confirmed. Support may record urgency, but Operations confirms whether the delivery can be committed. Staff must avoid promising same-day delivery until Operations has confirmed the route.

## Account Terms

Account terms are managed by Finance/Admin and Sales. Staff may explain general terms such as invoice billing, payment due dates, and credit hold process, but must not confirm an individual customer's live account balance or payment clearance without Finance/Admin confirmation.

## Product Recommendation Boundaries

Staff may recommend products based on approved catalog information and known customer usage. Staff must not provide professional medical, safety engineering, chemical handling, or regulatory advice. For clinics and industrial operators, the customer remains responsible for confirming product suitability for their regulated use.

## AI Assistant Notes

The assistant may answer general product category, service, substitution, urgent order, and account term questions from this FAQ. It must not confirm live stock, guarantee urgent delivery, match a marketplace price, or decide whether a regulated product is suitable for a customer's specific use. Clinic glove substitution should be treated as high risk and normally requires customer approval.
