# Data Handling and AI Usage Policy

**Owner:** Management Office  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-05-19  
**Classification:** Internal Use Only  

## Purpose

This policy explains how staff should handle company, customer, staff, supplier, invoice, payment, and operational data when using software tools and AI systems.

## Data Categories

Internal operating data includes SOPs, order notes, delivery notes, issue logs, supplier notes, pricing references, and internal templates.

Confidential customer data includes customer contacts, delivery addresses, account terms, invoices, payment status, order history, complaints, and special pricing.

Restricted staff or finance data includes salary information, personal identification details, bank information, disciplinary records, payment records, and management approvals.

## Approved Systems

Staff should keep customer and operational records in approved company systems, shared drives, and work accounts. Personal email, personal messaging, personal cloud storage, and unmanaged devices must not be used for confidential company data.

## Public AI Tools

Staff must not paste confidential customer data, invoice data, payment details, staff data, supplier pricing, internal margins, or private management decisions into public AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or other external services unless Management has approved a specific controlled workflow.

Staff may use public AI for generic drafting only when the prompt contains no customer, invoice, payment, staff, supplier, or confidential operational information.

Safe prompt example:

"Rewrite this generic reminder in a polite tone: Please send payment when convenient."

Unsafe prompt example:

"Customer ABC has invoice INV-1049 for RM4,210 overdue and says they paid Maybank yesterday. Draft a response."

## Private SOP Assistant

The private SOP assistant may answer from approved internal documents loaded into its knowledge base. It is designed to reduce repeated SOP questions and help staff find the right source document.

The assistant is not a live operational system. It does not know current order status, delivery ETA, payment clearance, stock quantity, or customer-specific account balance unless those facts are included in approved source documents. Staff must check the source system or owning team for live facts.

## Source Requirement

If an AI answer has no source, staff must not treat it as policy. They should ask the SOP owner, manager, or relevant function lead. Unsupported AI answers must not be used to approve refunds, credit notes, delivery commitments, pricing exceptions, leave, payment status, or customer account decisions.

If the assistant gives an answer that appears inconsistent with the source, staff should follow the approved source document and report the issue to the SOP owner or demo administrator.

## Staff Responsibilities

Staff are responsible for:

- Checking whether information is confidential before using AI.
- Removing customer and invoice identifiers from generic drafting prompts.
- Using approved company systems for customer and finance records.
- Asking a manager when they are unsure whether AI use is permitted.
- Avoiding final operational decisions based only on AI output.

## Incident Handling

If confidential data is accidentally pasted into an unapproved tool, the staff member must notify their manager and Management Office promptly. The report should include what data was shared, which tool was used, when it happened, and whether the data can be deleted or access revoked.

## AI Assistant Notes

The assistant may explain data categories, approved AI use, prohibited public AI use, source requirements, and what staff should do when an AI answer has no source. It must not process confidential invoice details, payment records, personal data, or customer-specific account status. It should clearly state that invoice data must not be pasted into ChatGPT or other public AI tools.
